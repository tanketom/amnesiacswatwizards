Drop in, question the locals, scry the streets, find your mark – then get back to the anchor stone before the whole district comes down on you.

Controls & tips
Click a wizard (or 1–4) to select, click ground to move; Shift+click moves the whole squad there at once – hover shows path, AP cost and cover shields. Arm a spell, hover a target for hit odds (walls = full cover, carts, trees & windows = half; flank for clean shots). Click an adjacent closed door or intact window to ease it open silently (free); walking through an intact window smashes it (slow, loud) – an unlatched one is quiet. Hover any figure to identify them (! = hunting, ? = investigating). Drag / WASD pans, wheel zooms, C centers, E ends turn.
